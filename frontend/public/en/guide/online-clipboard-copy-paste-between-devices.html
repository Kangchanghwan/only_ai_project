Clipboard Share

Online clipboard: copy on one device, paste on another

You have a chunk of text, a link or a screenshot on one device and need it on another. Emailing it to yourself leaves junk in your inbox, messengers compress images, and most "online clipboard" sites want you to type a code or create an account before they let you paste anything.

Clipboard Share works differently. Devices on the same Wi-Fi are grouped automatically the moment you open the page, so there is no code to exchange. Paste on one device and the item appears on the other a second later; when everyone closes the tab, the items are gone.

How to do it

  1. Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi network. A desktop on Ethernet counts as long as it is behind the same router.
  2. Open https://www.clipboardapp.org/en/ in a browser on each device. Devices on the same network are grouped automatically under the Same network tab. There is no code to type and no invite to send.
  3. On a computer, press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac) anywhere on the page to share whatever is on your clipboard: text, a link, or an image. On a phone, tap the Paste card once and allow clipboard access.
  4. For documents, photos or anything larger, use the Upload File card, or drag files onto the page on a computer. Files up to 100MB are sent without compression.
  5. The item shows up on the other device right away. Tap or click it to copy it to that device's clipboard, or use the download button to save a file.
  6. Once every device has left the page, the shared items are deleted automatically. Nothing is kept in an account because there is no account.

Good to know

  • Your clipboard is never read in the background. Only what you deliberately paste or upload is shared, and the browser asks for clipboard permission the first time on a phone.
  • Add the page to your home screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen on iPhone; the Chrome menu on Android) and it opens like an app. On Android it also appears in the share sheet of other apps.
  • If the two devices are on different networks, switch to the Everyone tab. Items there are visible to anyone with the service open, so keep personal files and one-time codes on the same network instead.
  • The interface is available in 21 languages and follows your system dark mode.

Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login and nothing to install. Open the page in Safari, Chrome or Edge on each device and start pasting.

How is this different from a regular online clipboard?

Most online clipboards give you a code or a URL you have to type on the other device. Here, devices on the same Wi-Fi are matched automatically, so the only step is pasting. It also handles files and QR hand-off, not just text.

Does it work across iPhone, Android, Windows and Mac?

Yes, in any combination. Anything with a current browser works, including iPads and Chromebooks.
